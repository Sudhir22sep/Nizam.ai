Shipping

Orders are prepared with care and dispatched using a tracked delivery service. Delivery times and charges are shown at checkout and may vary by destination and carrier.

Free standard shipping is available on eligible orders over ₹2,999. We aim to dispatch orders within 2–4 business days. You will receive tracking details when your parcel leaves our studio.

7-day returns

If an item is not right for you, contact us within 7 days of delivery. Items must be unused, unworn, unwashed, and returned with their original tags and packaging.

For hygiene reasons, some items may not be eligible for return. Your email will confirm eligibility and the next steps. Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method once the item is received and checked.

Need help?

Include your order number and a photo or description of the issue. Our customer care team will guide you through the return or answer delivery questions.
